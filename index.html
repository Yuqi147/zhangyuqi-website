Biography

My name is Yuqi Zhang, pronounced /y tɕʰi ʈʂɑŋ/ in Mandarin Chinese (with tones: Yǔqí Zhāng). However, I’m totally fine with different pronunciations of my first name, such as “Yuki,” “Yuchee,” “Youki,” or “Youchee.” Please feel free to pronounce it whichever way is easiest and most comfortable for you!

I grew up in Dalian (大连), a city in northeastern China. It was a beautiful coastal city where I spent my first 18 years there. Besides, I went to Canterbury, UK for my sixth year in primary school with my mother, who was a visiting scholar at University of Kent.

As an undergraduate at SUSTech in Shenzhen (深圳), I chose Geophysics as my major in the second year with focus on space physics. I worked with Prof. Shengyi Ye on planetary dusty physics, studying the dust environments around Mars and Jupiter. In spring semester of the third year, I went to UC Berkeley as an exchange student. My wife and I met in English of Academic Purpose course and got married in our fourth year.

I am now a PhD student in astronomy at Boston University, working with Prof. Wen Li on Earth's electron precipitation.

Outside the lab, I enjoy soccer, table tennis, badminton, strategy games, and cooking. Moreover, I love traveling.

Projects

CME-MOSS

Research-grade, multi-spacecraft CME/ICME analysis software that queries the CCMC DONKI CME catalog and propagates ICME wavefronts along Parker-spiral geometry with drag-based velocity evolution. A strict dynamic time-space test determines which spacecraft or planets are actually swept by the wavefront, with Parker-spiral overlays and in-situ solar-wind time series. Runs identically from a desktop GUI, the command line, or the Python API, and every search is fully reproducible from a JSON configuration.

Technology: Python pyspedas
Results: Encounter Detection · Wavefront Simulation · GUI/CLI
